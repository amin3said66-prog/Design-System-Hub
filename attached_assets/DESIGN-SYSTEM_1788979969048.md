## Arabic RTL

When RTL:

- text aligns naturally right
- navigation mirrors
- directional icons mirror where meaningful
- numeric content remains readable
- image composition should not be blindly mirrored
- brand logo should not be mirrored
- decorative photography should not be mirrored unless necessary

## Global CSS Rules

1. No !important unless there is a documented third-party conflict.
2. No duplicate selectors.
3. No duplicate color definitions.
4. No component-specific global selectors.
5. No random style overrides.
6. Prefer CSS variables + Tailwind.
7. Extract repeated UI patterns into components.
8. Keep globals small.
9. Keep tokens centralized.
10. Keep component styles local.

## Image Direction

Photography should be:

- cinematic
- natural
- high-end
- realistic
- Mediterranean/Red Sea light
- warm but restrained
- editorial
- premium hospitality

Egyptian visual references:

- Alexandria coast
- Red Sea turquoise
- El Gouna marinas
- Hurghada coastline
- Ain Sokhna
- Marsa Alam
- Nile at sunset

Avoid:
- stock-photo smiles
- fake luxury
- excessive saturation
- cliché tourist imagery
- artificial-looking AI photography

## Content Voice

English:
confident, refined, concise, sensory.

Arabic:
فصيح معاصر، راقٍ، واضح، غير متكلف.

Avoid slang in primary brand copy.

Good:
"رحلتك تبدأ من التفاصيل."

Bad:
"يلا نركب ونستمتع."

Good:
"ضيافة مصرية، بتفاصيل استثنائية."

Bad:
"أحلى رحلة في مصر."

---

## UX Principles

1. One primary action per section.
2. Reduce visual noise.
3. Let photography breathe.
4. Use whitespace as a luxury signal.
5. Use typography for hierarchy.
6. Never hide important information behind animation.
7. Keep booking/request actions obvious.
8. Make mobile equal to desktop in quality.
9. RTL is a first-class experience.
10. Accessibility is part of the design system.
